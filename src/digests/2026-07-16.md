📅 Daily Digest — July 16, 2026

**Global Macro & Markets**
The IMF's July WEO Update, subtitled "Crosscurrents of War and Technology," held global growth steady at 3.0% for 2026 but raised its inflation forecast from 4.1% to 4.7%, as the Middle East conflict's energy shock stalls the disinflation trend that had been running since early 2024. The divide is widening: energy importers and developing economies are absorbing the hit while countries integrated into the AI technology supply chain are getting a demand lift. New Fed Chair Kevin Warsh compounded the uncertainty by scrapping traditional forward guidance entirely at the June meeting, leaving markets to navigate on pure data dependence. [IMF World Economic Outlook Update, July 2026](https://www.imf.org/en/publications/weo/issues/2026/07/08/world-economic-outlook-update-july-2026)

**Enterprise AI & AI Strategy**
The Future of Life Institute's Summer 2026 AI Safety Index found no company worthy of better than a C+, with Anthropic topping the rankings, OpenAI and Google DeepMind at C, Meta at D+, and xAI, DeepSeek, and Mistral receiving failing grades. More significant than the scores: the report documents that all four major Western labs have weakened or eliminated commitments to pause development near danger thresholds, and every company that had previously prohibited military use has reversed that policy. [AI Safety Index — Summer 2026](https://futureoflife.org/ai-safety-index-summer-2026/)

**AI Tools & Developer Utilities**
China's Interim Measures for the Administration of Anthropomorphic AI Interaction Services took effect July 15, immediately forcing ByteDance's Doubao and Alibaba's Qwen to kill AI agent creation features — not just new agents, but all existing user-created agents stopped functioning simultaneously. On the same day, Meta launched its Business Agent Platform, targeting enterprise deployment of agents across WhatsApp, Messenger, and Instagram, backed by a $145B infrastructure commitment and a $27B compute capacity deal with Nebius. The juxtaposition of one major market closing the door on agents while another opens it underscores how fragmented the global AI agent landscape is becoming. [TechNode — China AI agent shutdowns](https://technode.com/2026/07/06/bytedances-doubao-and-alibabas-qwen-to-shut-down-ai-agent-features-on-july-15/)
