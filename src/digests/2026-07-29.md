**Enterprise AI & AI Strategy**

Deloitte's State of AI in the Enterprise 2026 report finds 84% of AI-investing organizations now report positive ROI — a decisive shift from the "promising but unproven" narrative of 2024. The more telling signal, though, is how companies are failing: Uber exhausted its entire 2026 AI budget within four months, and Starbucks scrapped its AI inventory system after nine months. The pattern suggests enterprises that treat AI as a procurement decision rather than an operational redesign are burning capital without durable returns.

**AI Tools & Developer Utilities**

Microsoft officially merged Semantic Kernel and AutoGen into **Microsoft Agent Framework 1.0** earlier this year, consolidating its fragmented agentic tooling into a single opinionated stack — with the original AutoGen now in maintenance mode. The broader framework landscape is consolidating around a short list: LangGraph for control-heavy stateful workflows, CrewAI for role-based multi-agent orchestration, and Google ADK for teams already in the Google ecosystem. Pydantic AI V2 and LlamaIndex Workflows 1.0 both hit stable releases in June, signaling the agentic layer is maturing past early experimentation.
