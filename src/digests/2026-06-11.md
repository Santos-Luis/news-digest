📅 Daily Digest — June 11, 2026

**Software Engineering & Architecture**
Sonar held a live demo today showcasing its Gitar integration, the AI code review platform it acquired in May. The demo focused on the combined product's core proposition for the agentic era: automated quality gates that engage from the moment an AI agent starts writing code through merge — catching CI failures, proposing fixes, and enforcing standards inside GitHub and GitLab PR workflows without requiring developer intervention at each step. The integration is built specifically for the problem Gartner flagged this week: that 60% of agentic AI projects are on track to fail in 2026 due to data quality and verification gaps. [Sonar Acquires Gitar](https://www.sonarsource.com/company/press-releases/sonar-acquires-gitar/)

**Global Macro & Markets**
May PPI came in well above expectations this morning — headline +1.1% month-over-month versus the 0.7% consensus, with annual wholesale inflation running at 6.5%, the hottest reading since late 2022 — sending short-end yields sharply higher and cementing the case for a Fed hike later this year. By afternoon, markets reversed course: President Trump signaled a deal with Iran was close and called off scheduled strikes, triggering a relief rally in equities and pulling the 10-year yield back more than 6 basis points to 4.48%. The whipsaw — hot inflation in the morning, geopolitical de-escalation by the close — encapsulates the split-signal environment that has made 2026 one of the more difficult years to trade. [BLS — May PPI Release](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/ppi_06112026.htm)

**Enterprise AI & AI Strategy**
Gartner published new data this week putting a number on the enterprise AI execution gap: 60% of agentic AI projects are projected to fail in 2026, primarily due to data immaturity rather than model capability. The underlying diagnosis is structural — nearly 48% of organizations have deployed AI without redesigning the workflows or roles around it, and only 12% report workflow transformation at scale. The pattern is consistent with what JPMorgan, in its $19.8 billion AI infrastructure commitment, identified as the distinguishing factor between AI experiments and AI infrastructure: governance and integration work, not model selection. [2026 Enterprise AI Predictions](https://www.informationweek.com/machine-learning-ai/2026-enterprise-ai-predictions-fragmentation-commodification-and-the-agent-push-facing-cios)

**AI Tools & Developer Utilities**
Microsoft's June 2026 Windows 11 update shipped several features aimed directly at developers working with AI agents: WSL Containers bring lightweight Linux sandbox environments to Windows without requiring a full VM, an Intelligent Terminal integrates AI agents for context-aware assistance directly in the shell, and Task Manager gains NPU tracking columns for per-process neural processing unit monitoring. The NPU visibility is the most forward-looking change — as on-device model inference expands, developers need the same profiling instrumentation for NPU workloads that they've had for CPU and GPU for years.
