**Enterprise AI & AI Strategy**
August 2 marked the EU AI Act's first real enforcement moment: the European Commission's AI Office is now actively fining companies for transparency violations — failing to disclose chatbot-as-AI status, unlabeled deepfakes, and AI-generated content lacking machine-readable markers — with penalties up to €15 million or 3% of global turnover. More than 180 organizations signed a voluntary Code of Practice in advance, but US companies serving EU customers are finding their exposure larger than anticipated. [European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-starts-enforcing-ai-act-rules-and-new-transparency-requirements-2-august)

**AI Tools & Developer Utilities**
Anthropic's Claude Opus 5, released in late July, is now the top-ranked model in August 2026 developer tool surveys, with Claude Code on Opus 5 leading for terminal-first agentic coding. Its standout new capability is an effort toggle — low, medium, or high — that lets users directly trade cost for reasoning depth per task, paired with a 1M-token context window and 128k output for long-horizon agent runs.
