**Software Engineering & Architecture**

Microsoft's Work IQ APIs went generally available on June 16, giving agents programmatic access to business context across email, calendar, meetings, chats, and line-of-business systems. Paired with major upgrades to Microsoft Foundry—hosted agents, memory, tracing, evaluations, and an Agent Optimizer—the platform layer for production-grade enterprise agents is maturing rapidly. The message from Redmond is that the scaffolding problem is largely solved; what remains is integration discipline. **Enterprise AI & AI Strategy**

GitHub confirmed yesterday it had to tap AWS capacity after AI coding agents pushed the platform past its SLA thresholds, with AI-opened pull requests surging from ~4 million in September 2025 to more than 17 million in March 2026—a 325% increase in six months. The incident highlights a structural engineering challenge: agents operate 24/7 at machine speed, running CI pipelines and pushing commits continuously, and existing infrastructure was sized around human usage patterns. [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318481/20260616/githubs-ai-agent-crisis-forces-microsoft-tap-aws-outages-break-enterprise-slas.htm)
