**AI Tools & Developer Utilities**
Claude Code became accessible via web browser and iPhone this week, removing the terminal-only constraint and letting developers run agentic coding sessions from any device. Separately, Claude Memory added a cross-platform sync layer carrying personalization and conversation context across Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google surfaces — a sign that personal AI identity is starting to decouple from any single provider. **Enterprise AI & AI Strategy**
Google cut its AI Ultra subscription from $250 to $100 per month following Google I/O, bundling in 5× higher usage limits, 20 TB of storage, YouTube Premium, and beta access to Gemini Spark — a 24/7 personal AI agent. The aggressive repricing signals that the premium AI subscription market is entering a consolidation phase where labs must justify consumer spend against each other rather than against the absence of AI. [Google Blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/ai/google-io-2026-all-our-announcements/)

**Global Macro & Markets**
US equity markets closed the week near record highs as AI earnings momentum continued to offset energy price headwinds and bond market volatility. With the 30-year Treasury yield having touched 5.2% earlier in the month, investors are watching both Fed signals and the US-Iran diplomatic track closely ahead of the next PCE data release.
