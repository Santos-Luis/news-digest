📅 Daily Digest — May 28, 2026

**Software Engineering & Architecture**
Google I/O 2026 delivered a dense slate of developer announcements headlined by Gemini 3.5 Flash — which Google claims outperforms Gemini 3.1 Pro on nearly every benchmark while running four times faster — and Google Antigravity 2.0, an agent-first platform that takes an idea and produces a production-ready app with integrated deployment and agent management. Also notable: Managed Agents in the Gemini API now let developers spin up a reasoning, tool-using agent in an isolated Linux environment with a single API call, a direct counter to Anthropic's own Managed Agents offering. [Google Blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/google-io-2026-developer-highlights/)

**Global Macro & Markets**
Oil settled below $89/barrel this week as the U.S. and Iran reported progress toward reopening the Strait of Hormuz, though markets remained cautious after the sides stalled on Tehran's enriched uranium stockpile and transit toll demands. Equities held near record highs but drifted sideways on the ambiguity, while a sharp shift in Fed futures — implied probability of a 2026 rate hike jumped from 0.8% to nearly 15% in a month — added a second layer of uncertainty beneath the surface calm. [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-26/asian-stocks-set-to-rise-as-iran-deal-hopes-hold-markets-wrap)

**Enterprise AI & AI Strategy**
A new HCLTech report warns that 43% of enterprise AI initiatives are at risk of failure, with nearly half of leaders now expecting measurable ROI within 18 months — a window most programs are not designed to meet. The same week, EY and Microsoft announced a $1B+ expansion of their alliance explicitly aimed at moving enterprises beyond experimentation, targeting the implementation gap that the HCLTech data quantifies: only 27% of executives say they have a comprehensive AI strategy, and just 20% believe their workforce is genuinely AI-ready. [HCLTech / PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/hcltech-report-warns-43-of-enterprise-ai-initiatives-may-fail-as-leaders-face-shrinking-timelines-for-impact-302777842.html)

**AI Tools & Developer Utilities**
Anthropic has quietly pivoted toward vertical agents, having shipped ten finance-specific agents in early May (deployable inside Claude Code, Claude Cowork, or headless via API) and following that with Claude for Legal on May 12, which plugs directly into law firm tooling and signed up 20,000 lawyers at launch with early adoption from Freshfields. The pattern — frontier model wrapped in domain-specific context and routed through existing professional workflows rather than a new interface — is emerging as the repeatable enterprise go-to-market playbook.
