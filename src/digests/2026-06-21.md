**Global Macro & Markets**

Iran announced the closure of the Strait of Hormuz on June 20, citing Israeli military actions — a move already being called the largest energy supply disruption since the 1970s oil crisis. At its peak, Brent crude hit $126/barrel, and the EIA now forecasts U.S. average gasoline prices nearly $1/gallon higher than its February outlook. Stock markets fell globally and bond markets sold off, while economists warn the stagflationary dynamics — rising energy costs colliding with slowing growth — could force central banks to reverse any planned rate cuts. **Enterprise AI & AI Strategy**

Anthropic has overtaken OpenAI in U.S. business AI adoption for the first time, with 34.4% of companies on Ramp's platform paying for Claude vs. 32.3% for ChatGPT — a dramatic reversal after Anthropic quadrupled its business adoption in a single year while OpenAI grew by just 0.3%. Claude Code is the primary engine behind the surge, becoming the fastest-growing product in Anthropic's history. The same Ramp report that crowns the new leader flags real fragility: compute constraints and consumption-based pricing that has already burned companies like Uber through their entire 2026 AI budgets by April.
