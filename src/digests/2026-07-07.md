**Software Engineering & Architecture**

Engineering orgs at Uber, DoorDash, and Cloudflare are now running AI-driven governance layers that evaluate code and infrastructure changes before implementation, using agents to check for architectural coherence, compliance, and safety while preserving human approval authority. The pattern represents a new layer in the software delivery pipeline—AI-as-auditor sitting between proposal and deployment rather than just accelerating authorship. **Global Macro & Markets**

Seven OPEC+ members—including Saudi Arabia, Russia, and Iraq—agreed Sunday to raise August production by 188,000 barrels per day, their fifth consecutive monthly increase, sending Brent crude to around $71.70 and near pre-conflict lows. The cartel is explicitly prioritizing market share over price support as Strait of Hormuz shipping steadily recovers from disruptions tied to the US-Israel-Iran conflict earlier this year. This follows AWS's own $1 billion internal commitment to the same problem and signals that hyperscalers now see professional deployment services as the decisive battleground, as enterprises struggle to move AI from pilot to production at viable cost.
