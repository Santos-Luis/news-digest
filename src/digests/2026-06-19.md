📅 Daily Digest — June 19, 2026

**Software Engineering & Architecture**

Vercel launched "eve" at its Ship 26 conference in London, an open-source TypeScript framework that treats every AI agent as a directory of files—`instructions.md` for the system prompt, typed tool files, and Markdown skill playbooks—compiling to a durable service on Vercel Functions with session state, sandboxed compute, and OpenTelemetry baked in. The framing is deliberate: Vercel wants eve to be to agents what Next.js is to web apps, and the numbers justify the bet—agents now trigger roughly 29% of deployments on Vercel, up from under 3% a year ago. [Vercel Launches Eve, an Open-Source Agent Framework](https://thenewstack.io/vercel-launches-eve-an-open-source-framework-that-treats-agents-as-directories/)

**Global Macro & Markets**

US equity markets rallied on June 18 after initially selling off on the Fed's decision to hold rates steady at 3.5–3.75% while removing its projected 2026 rate cut entirely from the dot plot—several FOMC members now see a hike as the likelier next move. The Russell 2000 led with a 2.1% gain and the S&P 500 closed up 1.1%, suggesting markets are pricing hawkishness as a sign of underlying resilience rather than a growth threat. All US exchanges are closed today, June 19, in observance of Juneteenth. **AI Tools & Developer Utilities**

Anthropic quietly reversed course on its June 15 Agent SDK billing overhaul: the planned move of Agent SDK, `claude -p`, and GitHub Actions usage to a separate monthly credit pool is not going live. Developers on all subscription tiers continue drawing Agent SDK usage from their normal limits—a relief for heavy users who had been bracing for the new per-user caps ($20 on Pro, up to $200 on Max 20x).
