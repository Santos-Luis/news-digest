📅 Daily Digest — 2026-05-20

**Software Engineering & Architecture**
Coder has launched Coder Agents in beta — a self-hosted, model-agnostic platform that lets enterprise teams run AI coding agents entirely on their own infrastructure, delegating tasks like writing code, generating tests, and opening PRs through a conversational interface. The launch comes alongside a broader product restructuring into three layers: Agents, AI Governance, and Workspaces, responding to research showing 70% of companies are deploying AI agents on infrastructure never designed to support them. [InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/05/coder-agents-self-hosted-ai/)

**Global Macro & Markets**
All eyes are on Nvidia tonight: the company reports Q1 FY2027 earnings after market close with consensus expecting $78.5B in revenue — a ~78% year-over-year jump — driven almost entirely by data center demand from hyperscalers who have committed over $725B in AI capex for 2026. The report is widely considered the single most important earnings print of the year, as it will either validate or complicate the AI infrastructure spending thesis that has driven markets since late 2024. [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/05/13/nvidia-reports-its-fiscal-2027-q1-earnings-may-20/)

**Enterprise AI & AI Strategy**
Gartner is warning that by 2027, half of enterprises without a people-centric AI strategy will lose their top AI talent to competitors — a striking finding given that only 27% of executives currently have a comprehensive AI strategy and just 20% believe their workforce is truly AI-ready. The forecast lands as a separate Writer.com report shows 79% of organizations now face AI adoption challenges, up double digits from 2025, with 54% of C-suite executives saying AI adoption is actively dividing their companies internally. [Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-05-13-gartner-predicts-by-2027-50-percent-of-enterprises-without-a-people-centric-ai-strategy-will-lose-their-top-ai-talent)

**AI Tools & Developer Utilities**
Anthropic temporarily raised Claude Code weekly usage limits by 50% through July 13, stacking on top of the existing doubled 5-hour limit — an unusually developer-friendly move that signals aggressive adoption push. Meanwhile, Snyk announced a partnership integrating its security scanning directly into Claude-based workflows, joining Opsera's similar Cursor integration, as security tooling vendors race to embed into AI coding pipelines before access patterns consolidate.
