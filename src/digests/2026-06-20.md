📅 Daily Digest — June 20, 2026

**Software Engineering & Architecture**

Anthropic this week shipped Workload Identity Federation to the Claude Platform, replacing static API keys with short-lived, scoped credentials issued at request time — eliminating an entire class of credential leak risk for teams running AI workloads in production. Alongside it came enterprise-managed MCP connector access starting with Okta, letting admins provision connectors once so users get zero-touch access on first login across Claude chat, Claude Code, and Cowork. The move signals a maturing security posture as agentic workloads become production infrastructure rather than experiments. **Global Macro & Markets**

New Fed Chair Kevin Warsh held rates steady at 3.50–3.75% on June 17, but the bigger story is the market repricing: investors have flipped from pricing in two cuts this year to now leaning toward a hike, driven by the FOMC's upward revision of 2026 PCE inflation to 3.6% and persistent energy-market disruption from the Middle East conflict. The 30-year Treasury briefly topped 5.19% in May — its highest since before the financial crisis — and the long end has barely retreated since, as fiscal and inflation risks keep long-duration sellers in control. [June 2026 Fed Dot Plot, BondSavvy](https://www.bondsavvy.com/fixed-income-investments-blog/fed-dot-plot)

**Enterprise AI & AI Strategy**

A Publicis Sapient survey of 1,550 AI decision-makers, released June 17 at VivaTech in Paris, found that 73% of enterprises now use AI regularly — yet only 10% describe it as core to how their business runs, and 79% report significant adoption challenges, up double digits from 2025. The report lands as Gartner simultaneously forecasts that over 40% of agentic AI projects will be cancelled by 2027, pointing to a governance gap: 67% of executives believe their company has already suffered a data leak from unapproved AI tools while 36% have no formal plan for supervising agents. The picture is one of broad but shallow adoption racing ahead of the organizational infrastructure needed to sustain it. [Publicis Sapient 2026 Enterprise AI Report](https://www.marketscale.com/industries/software-and-technology/publicis-sapients-2026-enterprise-ai-report-finds-wide-adoption-but-only-10-say-its-core-to-operations)

**AI Tools & Developer Utilities**

Anthropic's 2026 Agentic Coding Trends Report documents a structural shift in how software gets built: coding agents are moving from single-turn autocomplete to long-running, tool-wielding workflows that can research, write, debug, and deploy across multi-file projects. Demand for the Claude Agent SDK — rebranded from the Claude Code SDK last September — has surged from roughly 50 monthly searches in May 2025 to nearly 15,000 in April 2026, tracking the broader industry move toward agent-native development toolchains. Claude Code also shipped a round of quality-of-life fixes this week, including cleaner auto mode safety, better subagent reliability, and removal of startup clutter.
