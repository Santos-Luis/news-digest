📅 Daily Digest — June 1, 2026

**Software Engineering & Architecture**
Anthropic published its 2026 Agentic Coding Trends Report, finding the developer role has structurally shifted from writing code to orchestrating agents — a trend accelerating faster than most predicted. Gartner now projects 75% of developers will spend more time architecting and orchestrating than writing code directly by year-end. The report highlights that multi-agent workflows are becoming the default pattern for complex tasks, not an experimental one. [2026 Agentic Coding Trends Report](https://resources.anthropic.com/hubfs/2026%20Agentic%20Coding%20Trends%20Report.pdf)

**Global Macro & Markets**
Escalating Middle East tensions are forcing a significant repricing of Fed policy expectations. Oil surged to $107/barrel (WTI) and $119/barrel (Brent) as hopes for a US-China brokered resolution to Strait of Hormuz disruptions collapsed following the recent summit. Rate cut probabilities have now cratered — J.P. Morgan sees the Fed on hold through all of 2026, with a 25bps hike now the base case for Q3 2027. [Rate Cut Expectations Collapse as Oil Shocks Reset Fed Path](https://www.stonex.com/en-us/insights/rate-cut-expectations-collapse-as-oil-shocks-reset-fed-path/)

**Enterprise AI & AI Strategy**
A new report from Writer finds that 79% of organizations now face challenges adopting AI — a double-digit jump from 2025 — even as investment continues to climb. The core tension: individual productivity gains are measurable and real, but only 29% of companies see meaningful ROI from generative AI and 23% from agents. Separately, OpenAI disclosed that enterprise now accounts for over 40% of its revenue and is on pace to match consumer revenue by end of year, suggesting the gap between vendors winning and enterprises struggling to operationalize AI is widening. [Enterprise AI adoption in 2026: Why 79% face challenges](https://writer.com/blog/enterprise-ai-adoption-2026/)

**AI Tools & Developer Utilities**
Microsoft is canceling most internal Claude Code licenses with a hard June 30 deadline, redirecting engineers to GitHub Copilot CLI — a striking move given Claude Code had grown highly popular internally over the past six months. The driver is cost: token-based pricing was generating per-engineer bills of $500–$2,000/month, which became unsustainable at Microsoft's scale approaching fiscal year-end. Claude models themselves remain accessible through Copilot CLI, so this is less about Anthropic vs. Microsoft and more about the emerging tension between raw AI capability and enterprise cost governance. [Microsoft cancels Claude Code licenses, shifting developers to GitHub Copilot CLI](https://www.windowscentral.com/microsoft/microsoft-cancels-claude-code-licenses-shifting-developers-to-github-copilot-cli-a-move-likely-driven-by-financial-motives)
