📅 Daily Digest — July 15, 2026

**Software Engineering & Architecture**

GitHub published a detailed breakdown of how it cut token costs in agentic CI workflows by up to 62% through a combination of daily auditor and optimizer agents, aggressive MCP tool pruning (unused tool schemas were adding 10–15 KB per API call), and swapping some MCP calls for the `gh` CLI. The write-up is a practical template for any team running agent-heavy pipelines, and comes as GitHub transitions Copilot to usage-based billing tied to actual token consumption.

[GitHub Slashes Agent Workflow Token Spend up to 62%](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/05/github-agentic-token-savings/)

**Global Macro & Markets**

The US-Iran conflict over the Strait of Hormuz escalated sharply on July 13–14, with Iran striking three tankers and the US reimposing a naval blockade. Brent crude surged to nearly $87/barrel — a one-month high — after a 9.6% single-day spike, as Hormuz transits fell more than 50% week-over-week. The renewed energy shock is pushing rate-cut bets further out, with the 10-year Treasury yield climbing to 4.61% and the World Bank now projecting global growth of just 2.5% for 2026.

**Enterprise AI & AI Strategy**

The enterprise AI ROI reckoning is arriving: Uber exhausted its entire 2026 AI budget within four months, and Starbucks discontinued its AI inventory system after nine months. Against that backdrop, Microsoft launched the $2.5B "Microsoft Frontier Company" — 6,000 industry and engineering experts dedicated to turning enterprise AI investments into measurable outcomes — while analysts note that only 42% of companies say their AI strategy is "highly prepared," with confidence collapsing when questions turn to governance, data management, and talent.

**AI Tools & Developer Utilities**

Anthropic launched Claude Sonnet 5 with a 1M token context window and 128k max output tokens, alongside a significant Claude Code update that makes background agents substantially more autonomous: sessions that finish code work in a worktree now automatically commit, push, and open a draft PR rather than pausing for input.
