📅 Daily Digest — July 5, 2026

**Software Engineering & Architecture**

ProGPU, a GPU-first UI framework for .NET built on WebGPU and Silk.NET, is drawing attention on Hacker News for its architectural bet: route every layer of the UI stack—vector rendering, text layout, composition, effects—directly to the GPU and eliminate the CPU from the hot path entirely. With 1,158 commits and a modular six-layer design, it's the most serious attempt yet at making GPU-native desktop UIs viable outside of game engines. [github.com/wieslawsoltes/ProGPU](https://github.com/wieslawsoltes/ProGPU)

**Enterprise AI & AI Strategy**
