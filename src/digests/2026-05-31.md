**Software Engineering & Architecture**

Cognition AI closed a $1B+ Series D at a $26B valuation this week, with Devin's enterprise revenue exploding from $37M to $492M in a single year. The raise reflects a widening bet that agent-first architecture—where AI operates autonomously in long-horizon tasks—will ultimately outcompete IDE-embedded copilots. **Global Macro & Markets**

The global composite PMI dropped to 47.5 in May—its lowest reading in 31 months—driven largely by fallout from the Middle East conflict, which is now dragging real output into contraction territory across Germany, France, Italy, and the UK. Meanwhile, US Fed futures have shifted sharply: markets now price a 74.5% chance of rates staying flat through year-end, with rate-cut expectations pushed out to mid-2027 under the new Fed chair. **Enterprise AI & AI Strategy**

Gartner issued a warning this month that by 2027, half of enterprises lacking a people-centric AI strategy will lose their top AI talent to competitors. The backdrop: 79% of organizations report challenges adopting AI—up double digits from 2025—even as 59% spend over $1M annually on AI technology. Three-quarters of executives admit their AI strategy is "more for show" than actionable internal guidance. [Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-05-13-gartner-predicts-by-2027-50-percent-of-enterprises-without-a-people-centric-ai-strategy-will-lose-their-top-ai-talent)
