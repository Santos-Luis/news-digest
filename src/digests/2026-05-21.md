**Enterprise AI & AI Strategy**
OpenAI published a detailed argument for what it calls the "next phase of enterprise AI," claiming the bottleneck has shifted from model access to deployment infrastructure and that most enterprises are still running disconnected point solutions rather than integrated agent workflows. The framing lands as Deloitte's 2026 AI report finds just 29% of organizations see meaningful ROI from generative AI and 95% of pilots still fail to reach production — underscoring that the gap between enterprise AI ambition and operational reality hasn't closed. [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/next-phase-of-enterprise-ai/)

**AI Tools & Developer Utilities**
Anthropic updated Claude Managed Agents this month with three significant features: "Dreaming" (a scheduled process that reviews session history to improve agent memory over time), parallel multi-agent orchestration where a lead agent delegates to specialist sub-agents sharing a file system, and Outcomes (a rubric-graded loop that sends agents back to revise until a quality bar is met). Together these ship the scaffolding for long-running, self-correcting agent workflows without requiring developers to host their own orchestration infrastructure. [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/05/07/anthropic-updates-claude-managed-agents-with-three-new-features/)
