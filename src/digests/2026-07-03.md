📅 Daily Digest — July 3, 2026

**Software Engineering & Architecture**
GitHub reports cutting token costs in agentic CI workflows by up to 62% by pruning unused MCP tools, swapping some MCP calls for the `gh` CLI, and running daily "auditor" and "optimizer" agents — a telling signal that teams are now actively tuning the economics of AI-driven pipelines rather than just adopting them. Separately, the Agent Development Kit for Go 2.0 launched with a graph-based workflow engine and built-in human-in-the-loop orchestration, expanding serious agentic tooling beyond Python. **Global Macro & Markets**
The Trump administration declined to renew USMCA on its July 1 deadline, initiating bilateral renegotiation with Mexico while leaving Canada's status unresolved — adding fresh trade policy uncertainty just as markets brace for July 9, when a batch of reciprocal tariffs are set to revert to higher levels unless extended. The Section 122 tariffs (the 10% global levies imposed after the Supreme Court struck down IEEPA tariffs in February) also expire July 24, creating a concentrated window of market risk around trade policy. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/01/trump-usmca-canada-mexico-trade-treaty.html)

**Enterprise AI & AI Strategy**
Together AI closed an $800M Series C at an $8.3B valuation, led by Aramco Ventures with participation from Nvidia, Salesforce Ventures, and General Catalyst, for its platform enabling enterprises to train and serve open-source models — one of the largest pure-infrastructure AI rounds of the year. The deal reflects a strong enterprise bet on model-stack ownership over API dependence, as token cost overruns (Uber reportedly burned its entire 2026 AI budget by April) make pricing control increasingly strategic. [Tech Startups](https://techstartups.com/2026/07/01/venture-capital-startup-funding-roundup-july-1-2026/)

**AI Tools & Developer Utilities**
Anthropic launched Claude Sonnet 5, its most agentic model to date, with a 1M token context window and 128K max output tokens, at introductory pricing of $2/$10 per MTok through August 31. Alongside it, Claude Managed Agents entered public beta with scheduling and authenticated CLI access, and Anthropic debuted Claude Science — an auditable AI workbench covering the full research lifecycle from literature review to manuscript.
