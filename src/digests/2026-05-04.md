**Software Engineering & Architecture**
The Pragmatic Engineer's 2026 survey paints a striking picture: 95% of developers now use AI tools weekly, 75% use AI for at least half their engineering work, and Claude Code tops the adoption list. The shift is changing role definitions—engineers increasingly orchestrate rather than write, with staff-plus engineers leading agent adoption at 63.5%. The survey also flags a growing problem: 30% of respondents have hit usage limits, and cost concerns thread through the entire report.

**Global Macro & Markets**
Oil markets dipped slightly today as Trump announced plans to help stranded ships exit the Strait of Hormuz, with WTI crude at $101.65/barrel and Brent at $108.10—down from a peak of $126 in March. The ongoing US-Iran conflict has blockaded the strait since late February, trapping some 2,000 ships and 20,000 mariners and triggering the largest energy supply disruption since the 1970s. Markets remain cautious as investors await Friday's US jobs report and watch for signals on Fed rate decisions complicated by persistent inflation risks.

**Enterprise AI & AI Strategy**
OpenAI launched Workspace Agents, a successor to custom GPTs that plugs directly into enterprise tools like Slack and Salesforce, as it disclosed that enterprise revenue now exceeds 40% of total revenue and is trending toward parity with consumer. Separately, a Writer report found 79% of organizations struggle to adopt AI despite majority investing over $1M annually—only 29% report meaningful ROI from generative AI and 23% from agents. The gap between individual productivity wins and measurable business value remains the central challenge facing CIOs this year.
