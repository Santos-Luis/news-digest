📅 Daily Digest — July 24, 2026

**Software Engineering & Architecture**

GitLab's AI Accountability Report (1,528 developers across six countries) confirms the "AI Paradox" at scale: 80% of organizations adopted AI coding tools faster than they built governance policies, and 92% report governance challenges with AI-generated code. The bottleneck has migrated downstream — 85% say AI shifted pressure from writing to reviewing code, and 43% can no longer reliably distinguish AI-generated from human-written code in their own codebase. [GitLab IR](https://ir.gitlab.com/news/news-details/2026/GitLab-Research-Reveals-Organizations-Are-Generating-AI-Code-Faster-Than-They-Can-Control-It/default.aspx)

**Global Macro & Markets**

Today marks the scheduled expiration of the Section 122 blanket 10% tariff on virtually all US imports, imposed February 24 under a rarely-used 150-day presidential emergency trade authority. Congress is unlikely to extend it, but USTR's Section 301 replacement duties (10–12.5%, with no statutory sunset) are in the pipeline after a June determination and July public hearing — meaning duty burdens may shift rather than disappear. This lands during a rough week for equities: the S&P 500 is down ~2% and the Nasdaq off ~2.7% as markets confront sticky inflation and elevated real yields. [Statt](https://statt.com/blog/section-122-tariff-expiration-what-businesses-need-to-know/)

**Enterprise AI & AI Strategy**

The center of gravity in enterprise AI is visibly shifting from model selection to orchestration, governance, and ROI measurement. Despite 90% of organizations reporting that agentic AI is transforming workflows, only 18% say it's delivering significant revenue impact — while CFO scrutiny of AI budgets intensifies ahead of Q3 reviews. The organizations pulling ahead are building control planes and measurement frameworks tied to decision outcomes, not deployment counts. [MarketScale](https://www.marketscale.com/industries/software-and-technology/enterprise-ais-center-of-gravity-shifts-from-models-to-orchestration-governance-and-roi-clarity)
