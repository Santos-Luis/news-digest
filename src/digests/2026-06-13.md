**Enterprise AI & AI Strategy**

Microsoft on June 9 formally classified KPMG as a "Frontier Firm"—its designation for organizations that have rebuilt their operating models around AI rather than layering AI onto existing workflows—as part of a global rollout of Agent 365 and Copilot across KPMG's workforce. The designation matters as a template: Frontier Firm status comes with dedicated engineering support and early access to agentic product releases, creating a tiered ecosystem in which a handful of strategic partners compound capability advantages over slower-moving enterprises. [KPMG and Microsoft Scale AI Agents Globally](https://news.microsoft.com/source/2026/06/09/kpmg-and-microsoft-scale-trusted-enterprise-ai-agents-globally-through-deployment-of-agent-365-and-copilot/)

**AI Tools & Developer Utilities**

Anthropic's billing split goes live in two days: starting June 15, Agent SDK usage, headless `claude -p`, Claude Code GitHub Actions, and third-party apps authenticating through the SDK will draw from a new per-month credit pool ($20 on Pro, $100 on Max 5×, $200 on Max 20×) rather than shared interactive usage, billed at standard API rates with no rollover. Users need to claim their credit through their Claude account before the deadline—and decide whether to enable API-rate overflow billing so pipelines don't silently stop when the credit runs dry.
