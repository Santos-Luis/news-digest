**Software Engineering & Architecture**
GitLab's 2026 AI Accountability Report surfaces a productivity paradox: 78% of developers say they code faster with AI, yet overall software delivery velocity hasn't improved because testing, review, and governance bottlenecks downstream are absorbing the gains. The finding reframes the AI-in-engineering debate from "will developers write code faster" to "can organizations actually ship faster" — and right now, the answer is no.
