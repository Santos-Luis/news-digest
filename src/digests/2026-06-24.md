📅 Daily Digest — June 24, 2026

**Software Engineering & Architecture**
Microsoft and Replit announced a partnership aimed at compressing the path from idea to enterprise-grade production app "measured in hours rather than months," tying into Microsoft's Fabric and Azure stack. Separately, Anthropic's Claude Agent SDK — which shares the same harness as Claude Code — began drawing from a separate monthly Agent SDK credit on June 15, marking the tool's formal commercial billing launch and signaling increasing adoption by teams building production agents in Python and TypeScript. [Microsoft Azure Blog](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/blog/microsoft-build-2026-building-agentic-apps-with-microsoft-fabric-and-microsoft-databases/)

**Global Macro & Markets**
The OECD issued a warning that global growth is now projected to slow to 2.5% in 2026, citing an energy shock driven by the ongoing Middle East conflict as the primary culprit — with emerging markets facing their weakest per-capita income growth since the pandemic. The Federal Reserve held rates steady at 3.50–3.75%, but Chair Kevin Warsh's press conference was read as hawkish, with updated projections showing the median policymaker now expecting a modest tightening by year-end — a reversal from March's rate-cut outlook. Long-end bond yields across advanced economies have hit levels not seen in nearly 20 years. [OECD Press Release](https://www.oecd.org/en/about/news/press-releases/2026/06/global-economic-outlook-weakens-amid-energy-shock-and-rising-inflationary-pressures.html)

**Enterprise AI & AI Strategy**
CMU's Software Engineering Institute and Accenture released an AI Adoption Maturity Model this month — an empirically validated framework designed to help enterprises move past experimentation and scale AI with repeatable outcomes. The release comes as a new Writer report finds 79% of organizations face challenges adopting AI (up double digits from 2025), and only 10% say AI is truly core to how their business operates despite 73% reporting regular use across processes — a widening gap between deployment and real transformation. [SEI/Accenture Release](https://www.sei.cmu.edu/news/sei-and-accenture-release-ai-adoption-maturity-model-to-help-organizations-scale-ai-with-predictable-outcomes/)

**AI Tools & Developer Utilities**
Google sunset both Gemini CLI and Gemini Code Assist on June 18, consolidating its developer-facing AI tooling into a single harness called Antigravity — built on the Agent Development Kit (ADK) and powered by Gemini — marking a significant strategic consolidation. Meanwhile, the agent framework landscape is hardening into two clear camps: provider-native SDKs (Claude, OpenAI, Google Antigravity) optimized for a single model family, and cross-provider frameworks like LangGraph, CrewAI, and Pydantic AI, with Microsoft's merged AutoGen/Semantic Kernel hitting GA back in April.
