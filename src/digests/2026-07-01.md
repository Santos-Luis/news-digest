📅 Daily Digest — July 1, 2026

**Software Engineering & Architecture**
The 2025 DORA report finds that AI doesn't automatically improve software delivery — it acts as a multiplier, strengthening high-performing teams while exposing weaknesses in struggling ones. Organizations investing in platform engineering before layering in AI see significantly better outcomes, as structured platforms give AI tools a consistent foundation to scale across teams. The finding challenges the assumption that AI is a leveler; it's more of an accelerant. [InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/03/ai-dora-report/)

**Global Macro & Markets**
Markets enter the holiday-shortened week with the June payrolls report as the key catalyst, and Fed rate-hike expectations have sharply repriced — the implied probability of a hike at the July meeting jumped from 6.4% to 36.3% in a single week. Dollar strength continues to dominate FX, driven by higher-for-longer rate differentials vs. G10 peers, while oil price easing has shifted central bank attention toward growth dynamics. Emerging markets face the weakest per capita income growth since the pandemic as global growth is projected to slow to 2.5% this year. [Convera FX Outlook July 2026](https://convera.com/blog/market-insights/fx-research/monthly-global-fx-outlook/fx-outlook-july-2026/)

**Enterprise AI & AI Strategy**
A 2026 global enterprise AI report reveals a stark adoption-readiness gap: 73% of organizations report AI is used regularly or across most business processes, yet only 10% say it's core to how the business actually operates. Agentic AI has moved from experiment to deployment across legal, finance, and engineering functions, but the AI skills gap remains the leading barrier to deeper integration. Board-level AI value reporting — currently practiced by just 4% of companies — is expected to become a standard requirement for public companies by year-end. [Publicis Sapient](https://www.publicissapient.com/company/news/ai-adoption-enterprise-readiness-report-2026)
