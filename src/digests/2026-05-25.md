**Software Engineering & Architecture**
Google I/O 2026 (May 19) centered on agentic infrastructure for developers, with Antigravity 2.0 launching as a standalone desktop app for orchestrating parallel agents — one coding, another generating assets simultaneously — backed by built-in sandboxing and hardened Git policies. Google also proposed WebMCP as an open web standard that lets browser-based AI agents interact with structured tools, with a Chrome 149 origin trial starting now. Managed Agents in the Gemini API round out the release, letting developers spin up isolated Linux environments with a single API call. [Google Developers Blog](https://developers.googleblog.com/all-the-news-from-the-google-io-2026-developer-keynote/)

**Global Macro & Markets**
The S&P 500 has surged 6% in May after the Supreme Court ruled 6-3 that IEEPA does not authorize tariffs, unwinding most of April's shock — but the relief may be premature. PPI jumped to 6% annually in April (from 4% in March), CPI sits at 3.8%, and the Fed's first rate cut is now priced for mid-2027, keeping higher-for-longer dynamics firmly in place even as equity markets hit new highs on record S&P 500 margins of 13.4%. **Enterprise AI & AI Strategy**
New research from Writer surfaces a widening ROI chasm: 97% of companies deployed AI agents in the past year, yet 48% now call AI adoption "a massive disappointment" — up sharply from 34% last year — with only 29% reporting significant organizational ROI. The core blockers remain data infrastructure, governance, and change management, suggesting most enterprises jumped to agent deployment before building the foundations needed to capture value. [Writer](https://writer.com/blog/enterprise-ai-adoption-2026/)

**AI Tools & Developer Utilities**
Anthropic acquired Stainless for approximately $300M, absorbing the company that built all official Claude API SDKs and created the Model Context Protocol (MCP), which now sees 97 million monthly downloads and powers 10,000+ public servers — including integrations adopted by OpenAI and Google. The move gives Anthropic vertical control over the model, the connectivity standard, and the toolchain, while Stainless will wind down its hosted SDK generator and refocus entirely on Claude Platform. [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-acquires-stainless)
