📅 Daily Digest — July 25, 2026

**Software Engineering & Architecture**
GitLab's 2026 AI Accountability Report reveals an "AI Paradox": 78% of developers are writing code faster with AI tools, but overall software delivery hasn't accelerated because the bottleneck has shifted downstream — 85% say AI has moved the pressure from writing code to reviewing and validating it. More troubling, 43% of organizations can no longer reliably distinguish AI-generated from human-written code in their own codebase, and 80% adopted AI coding tools faster than governance policies could keep up. [GitLab 2026 AI Accountability Report](https://about.gitlab.com/resources/ai-accountability-survey-2026/)

**Global Macro & Markets**
The IMF's July 2026 World Economic Outlook Update holds global growth at 3.0% for the year but flags a more fragmented picture than April's forecast: energy-importing nations and vulnerable economies are being weighed down by ongoing conflict, while AI-driven demand is lifting countries integrated into the global tech value chain. Global disinflation has stalled, with officials raising their 2026 inflation projection and acknowledging a potential need for additional monetary tightening. [IMF WEO Update July 2026](https://www.imf.org/en/publications/weo/issues/2026/07/08/world-economic-outlook-update-july-2026)

**Enterprise AI & AI Strategy**
Enterprise AI's center of gravity has definitively shifted from model selection to orchestration, governance, and ROI clarity — the industry stopped chasing raw model size and started optimizing for task completion, cost, and reliability. Deloitte's State of AI in the Enterprise 2026 puts a number on the inflection point: 84% of organizations investing in AI now report positive ROI, a sharp contrast to the "promising but unproven" framing that dominated just two years ago. Multimodal capability (text, image, audio, video) is now the baseline expectation for new enterprise deployments. [Enterprise AI 2026: Orchestration, Governance, ROI](https://www.marketscale.com/industries/software-and-technology/enterprise-ais-center-of-gravity-shifts-from-models-to-orchestration-governance-and-roi-clarity)

**AI Tools & Developer Utilities**
Vercel shipped AI SDK 7 on June 25, bringing typed tool context, `WorkflowAgent` durability primitives, approvals, and built-in telemetry — a significant production-readiness upgrade for teams building agents on top of Next.js. Separately, on July 16 OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google simultaneously released upgraded coding models (including `claude-opus-4.7` and `gpt-5.5-pro`), and Microsoft's hosted agent runtime in Foundry reached general availability, completing a week that substantially raised the floor for what off-the-shelf agentic infrastructure can do. [Vercel AI SDK 7](https://www.developersdigest.tech/blog/vercel-ai-sdk-7-production-agents)
