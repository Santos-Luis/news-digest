**Global Macro & Markets**

The FOMC's 8-4 dissent at its April meeting (the most divided vote since 1992) signals that whoever replaces Powell will inherit a fractured committee with competing views on whether 3.5–3.75% is too tight or not tight enough. **AI Tools & Developer Utilities**
F5's 2026 State of Application Strategy Report, out today, finds that 78% of enterprises now run AI inference as a core operation and manage an average of seven AI models simultaneously — making model routing, traffic management, and inter-model security the new infrastructure bottleneck. The implication for developers is that "AI delivery" is becoming as much an ops discipline as a software one, with chained model routing introducing new attack surfaces whenever prompts and context move between models.
