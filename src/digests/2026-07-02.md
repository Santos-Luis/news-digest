📅 Daily Digest — July 2, 2026

**Software Engineering & Architecture**
A Pragmatic Engineer survey of 906 engineers finds that 95% now use AI tools at least weekly, and 75% use AI for half or more of their engineering work. Claude Code went from zero to the most-loved coding tool in eight months — 46% of respondents named it their favorite, against 19% for Cursor and 9% for GitHub Copilot. Agent adoption has reached 55%, up from near-zero 18 months ago. [The Pragmatic Engineer](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/ai-tooling-2026)

**Global Macro & Markets**
June payrolls drop today in a holiday-shortened week — consensus expects ~170,000 jobs — and the number carries unusually high stakes: markets have repriced Fed hike odds to 67% for September, up from ~20% a month ago, after core PCE hit 3.4% YoY in May, the highest since October 2023. New Fed Chair Kevin Warsh has effectively ended the rate-cut conversation, and a strong print could push hike pricing even further. The S&P 500 recently cleared 7,600 for the first time, making any downside payrolls surprise particularly sharp. [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/policy/articles/traders-fully-price-fed-rate-124726318.html)

**Enterprise AI & AI Strategy**
On June 5, President Trump signed NSPM-11, directing the U.S. military and intelligence community to aggressively accelerate AI adoption across warfighting and intelligence operations. The memo rescinds the Biden-era NSM-25, removes restrictions it characterized as ideological mandates, and gives the Pentagon 90 days to update its policy on autonomous weapons systems. Within 120 days, agencies must overhaul procurement processes to enable rapid onboarding of AI models from multiple vendors. [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/06/national-security-presidential-memorandum-nspm-11/)

**AI Tools & Developer Utilities**
Anthropic released Claude Sonnet 5 on June 30, making it the new default model across all plans, priced at $2/M input and $10/M output tokens through August 31. The model delivers near-Opus performance on agentic tasks — planning, tool use, coding — and Anthropic positions it as cheaper than GPT-5.5 and Gemini 3.1 Pro at comparable capability. The promotional pricing window is a clear signal that Anthropic is prioritizing scale of adoption ahead of a widely anticipated IPO. [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-sonnet-5)
