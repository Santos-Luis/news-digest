📅 Daily Digest — May 29, 2026

**AI Tools & Developer Utilities**
Anthropic released Claude Opus 4.8 yesterday, its most capable generally available model, anchored by a new "dynamic workflows" feature in Claude Code that lets agents spin up parallel subagents mid-task. The release also introduces mid-task system messages on the Messages API and an optional 2.5× fast mode, marking a meaningful shift toward long-running autonomous work. Pricing holds at $5/$25 per million tokens with up to 90% savings via prompt caching. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/28/anthropic-releases-opus-4-8-with-new-dynamic-workflow-tool/)

**Enterprise AI & AI Strategy**
Earlier this month OpenAI launched the OpenAI Deployment Company, a $4B majority-owned subsidiary backed by TPG, Bain Capital, and Brookfield, designed to embed specialized AI engineers directly inside client organizations. The move was anchored by the acquisition of Tomoro, a 150-person applied AI consulting firm whose clients include Fidelity, Virgin Atlantic, and Tesco — signaling that frontier AI labs are now competing head-on with systems integrators for enterprise transformation budgets. [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/tomoro-openai-deployment-company-consulting)

**Global Macro & Markets**
The 30-year US Treasury yield briefly hit 5.2% on May 19 — its highest since 2007 — as inflation fears driven by Middle East energy disruptions and a federal deficit projected to exceed $1.95 trillion combined to spook bond investors. Yields have since retreated toward 4.5% as energy prices fell and a US-Iran diplomatic track emerged, but markets remain on edge ahead of the PCE print, with roughly 50/50 odds now priced for a Fed rate hike by December. [CNN Business](https://www.cnn.com/2026/05/19/business/30-year-treasury-yield-bond-record)

**Software Engineering & Architecture**
Google I/O (May 19–20) introduced Antigravity 2.0 and its CLI, enabling developers to orchestrate specialized subagents for complex workflows with built-in terminal sandboxing and credential masking baked in. Google also previewed an Android Studio migration tool that automatically converts React Native and web-framework apps to native Kotlin, and updated its Android Bench LLM leaderboard to include open-weight models.
